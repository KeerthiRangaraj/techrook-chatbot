PART IV: LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY

Chapter 12: Leave Entitlements
Leave year: 1 January to 31 December. Leave prorated for mid-year joiners.

Casual Leave (CL): 12 days annual entitlement. No carry-forward; lapses 31 December. Not encashable. Accrual: 1 day per month, credited on 1st of each month.
CL Rules: Max 3 consecutive CL days. Cannot combine CL with PL. Cannot combine with public holiday to exceed 5 consecutive calendar days without Department Head approval. Apply on HR Portal before leave date. Emergency: notify manager by 10:00 IST and apply within 24 hours of return.

Privilege Leave (PL) / Earned Leave (EL): 15 days annual entitlement. Max 30 days carry-forward. Encashable on separation only: (Basic Salary per month / 26 working days) x PL days — calculated by Finance Department. Accrual: 3.75 days per quarter after probation confirmation.
PL Rules: Employees on probation do not earn PL. PL accrual begins from date of confirmation. Minimum 7 calendar days advance notice required. May combine with public holidays and CL with prior approval. Excess of 30 days carry-forward lapses at year end.

Sick Leave (SL): 10 days annual entitlement. Max 15 days carry-forward. Not encashable. Full quota credited 1 January; prorated for new joiners.
SL Rules: 3 or more consecutive SL days requires Medical Certificate from Registered Medical Practitioner submitted within 3 working days of return. HR may request certificate for any SL regardless of duration. If SL exhausted, PL applied automatically. If PL also exhausted, leave without pay (LWP) at HR discretion.

Chapter 13: Public and Festival Holidays
TechRook observes minimum 14 paid public holidays per calendar year.
Mandatory holidays: Republic Day (26 January), Holi, Good Friday, Ambedkar Jayanti (14 April), Labour Day (1 May), Eid ul-Fitr, Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Dussehra, Diwali (main day), Christmas (25 December), TechRook Foundation Day (14 March), 2 Regional Holidays (location-specific, announced by HR by 15 December of prior year).

PART V: COMPENSATION, PAYROLL, AND BENEFITS

Chapter 15: Salary Structure and Payroll
Salary Components:
Basic Salary: 40–50% of CTC; basis for PF, Gratuity, Leave Encashment. Fully taxable.
House Rent Allowance (HRA): 50% of Basic for metro cities (Hyderabad, Bangalore, Pune, Noida); 40% for non-metro. Partially exempt per IT Act.
Special Allowance: Balance of CTC after statutory deductions. Fully taxable.
Medical Allowance: INR 1,250 per month (fixed). Exempt up to INR 15,000 p.a.
Leave Travel Allowance (LTA): 1 month Basic Salary per year; claimable once per calendar year against travel bills. Exempt per IT Act.
Performance Bonus: 0–30% of Annual CTC; paid semi-annually based on KRA achievement. Fully taxable.

Salary Payment:
Salaries credited to ICICI Bank salary account on last working day of each calendar month.
If last working day falls on bank holiday, salary advanced to previous working day.
HR processes salaries by 25th of each month; Finance initiates transfer by 28th.
Pay slips available on TechRook HR Portal on or before 5th of following month.

Chapter 16: Statutory Benefits
Provident Fund (PF): 12% of employee Basic Salary deducted. TechRook contributes equal 12% Basic. Of TechRook 12%, 8.33% goes to Employees Pension Scheme (EPS). Maintained with EPFO Regional Office Hyderabad.

Employees State Insurance (ESIC): For employees with gross monthly salary INR 21,000 or below. Employee contribution: 0.75% gross. TechRook contribution: 3.25% gross. ESIC-covered employees not eligible for Group Mediclaim simultaneously.

Gratuity: All employees covered under Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 from date of joining. Eligible after 5 years continuous service. Formula: (Last drawn Basic Salary / 26) x 15 x completed years of service. Payable within 30 days of last working day.

Group Personal Accident Insurance: All employees L0 to L4 covered from date of joining. Coverage: INR 25 Lakhs per employee for accidental death and permanent total disability. Active 24 hours/day, 365 days/year, anywhere in world. Employee or nominee must notify HR within 24 hours of incident.

Group Mediclaim Insurance: All employees at L1 and above (and L0 converted to full-time) not covered under ESIC. Coverage: INR 5 Lakhs per annum per employee. Covers employee + 3 dependents (spouse, up to 2 children). Minimum 24-hour hospitalization required. Pre-existing conditions covered after 30-day waiting period.

PART VIII: LEGAL, CONDUCT, AND COMPLIANCE POLICIES

Chapter 26: Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy
Legal Basis: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013.
Coverage: All employees — permanent, contractual, intern, temporary — all locations including client sites, travel, and virtual.
ICC contact: posh.icc@techrook.in (monitored 24/7)
ICC composition at each office: 1 Presiding Officer (senior female employee L3+), 2 Internal Members, 1 External Member (NGO representative).
Complaint filing: Written complaint to ICC within 90 calendar days of incident. Include date, time, location, persons involved, witnesses.
ICC acknowledges within 3 working days.
Inquiry completed within 60 calendar days.
ICC findings submitted to HR and CEO within 10 working days of inquiry completion.
Complainant may request interim relief (transfer to different team).
Consequences: written warning, mandatory training, suspension without pay, demotion, transfer, or termination. False malicious complaints also subject to disciplinary action.
Confidentiality: identity of all parties strictly confidential. Disclosure punishable.
